# FidGames Master Plan

## Vision
FidGames is a keychain-sized fidget gaming device designed for ultra-short gaming sessions.

Core idea:
- Fun physical controls first
- Games second
- One-handed operation
- Endless score chasing
- Instant resume after locking

## Design Principles

1. Sessions last 30 seconds to 5 minutes.
2. No story and no campaign.
3. No "game completed" state.
4. Focus on high scores.
5. Device resumes exactly where the player left off.
6. One-handed play is preferred.
7. Exceptions are allowed when they significantly improve gameplay.
8. Haptics replace audio.
9. Physical control feel is as important as the games themselves.

## Target Experience

The device should be enjoyable:
- While playing
- While idly fidgeting
- While attached to keys
- While carried in a pocket

## Inputs

Primary:
- Analog magnetic self-centering slider
- Rear trigger button
- Lock button

Secondary:
- Gyroscope / accelerometer

## Gameplay Philosophy

Games should:
- Be instantly understandable
- Have simple controls
- Reward mastery
- Encourage "one more try"

## Example Game Categories

- Endless runner
- Endless stack builder
- Snake-inspired game
- Rhythm games
- Reaction games
- Balance games
- Physics toys
